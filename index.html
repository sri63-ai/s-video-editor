1. How to Upload Media Files

Starting your project takes just a few clicks. Simply tap the Upload Video button to select your high resolution footage, screen recordings, or audio files directly from your phone or computer. 2. Timeline Control & Unlimited PiP Overlays

The timeline sits right underneath your video preview player, and its where youll do most of your editing. You can easily drag and drop your clips, line up your audio tracks, and make clean, frame by frame cuts to piece your video together.

  • Video Track: Make clean, frame by frame cuts and chop out the mistakes. Then, just drag and drop your best shots to stitch your video together.
  • PiP Layering: Stack as many extra videos and photos on top of your main track as you want. It's perfect if you're adding a facecam for gaming videos, making reaction clips, or building a split screen collage.

Add background music and sound effects using our built in audio library, or upload your own custom tracks.

  • Designer Text Overlays: Drop in titles, lower thirds, or captions. You can fully customize the text by tweaking the fonts, colors, and formatting to match your style.

Everything you need to know about using S Studio & S Video Editor

Is S Video Editor completely free to use? ➕
Yes. All core editing features, AI tools, subtitle generation, and high-definition exports are completely free without hidden subscription paywalls, credit limits, or trial expirations.
Are my uploaded videos and photos stored on your servers? ➕
No. Your privacy is 100% protected. Everything processes locally directly inside your web browser. Your private videos, personal clips, and images are never stored or uploaded to external cloud servers.
Do exported videos have forced watermarks? ➕
Never. Every single exported video or edited photo is delivered completely clean without forced watermarks or brand overlays, keeping your content fully professional.
What video resolutions and formats can I export? ➕
You have total resolution freedom. You can export lightweight videos in 240p, 360p, or 480p for fast sharing, up to 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, and crisp 1440p (2K Ultra HD) in universal MP4 format.
How does the Auto AI Subtitles feature work? ➕
When you click the Subtitles button, our browser speech tool listens to your audio and generates readable captions automatically. You can also tweak text fonts, font sizes, colors, and box positioning with live preview.
Can I edit both videos and photos in one single workspace? ➕
Yes. S Studio includes a dedicated Photo Editing suite alongside the video editor. You can design YouTube thumbnails, remove photo backgrounds, and create graphic overlays without switching between multiple apps.
Do I need a high-end computer or graphics card to run S Studio? ➕
No expensive hardware is needed. The editor is engineered to run smoothly inside modern web browsers across everyday laptops, desktop PCs, and smartphones without heavy memory bottlenecks.
How can I blur sensitive details like faces, phone numbers, or license plates? ➕
Using our built-in Blur Mask slider and Mosaic tools, you can easily cover up private text, personal details, or moving faces anywhere across your video canvas.
Does S Studio support green screen (Chroma Key) background removal? ➕
Yes. The Chroma Key toggle allows you to knock out solid green or blue backdrops in one click and layer custom photos, gameplay videos, or graphics behind your subject.
Do I need to sign up or create an account to edit and export? ➕
