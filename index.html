S Studio Editing Tools – User Guide

Browser-Based Video & Photo Editing Workspace

S Studio (svideoeditor.com) allows you to edit videos and photos directly inside your web browser without downloading heavy desktop software. Built for content creators, students, and digital marketers, the platform delivers fast, accessible tools to cut, layer, color-grade, and export studio-ready projects on any device.

1. Uploading Media Files

Starting a project takes just a few clicks. Select the Upload Video or Upload Photo button to import raw camera clips, screen recordings, high-resolution images, or audio files directly from your phone or computer. Once your media loads, the full editing workspace activates immediately—processing everything locally on your device for instant responsiveness and complete privacy.

2. Multi-Track Timeline Control & Picture-in-Picture (PiP)

The multi-track timeline sits directly below your preview player, serving as your central editing workspace. Drag and drop clips, zoom in for frame-level precision, and align overlays effortlessly.

  • Main Video Track: Make frame-accurate razor cuts to remove mistakes, freeze key moments, and stitch your strongest takes together.
  • Auto-Stacking PiP Layering: Stack multiple video clips, logos, and images above your main track—ideal for gaming facecams, reaction videos, product callouts, or split-screen layouts.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Speed up your workflow using Space to Play/Pause and S to split clips at the playhead.

3. Audio Studio & Background Music Library

Give your edits crisp, balanced sound using dedicated audio track controls and mood-matched soundscapes:

  • Local Track Import & Audio Extraction: Upload custom voiceovers, sound effects, or music from your device—or detach audio directly from any video clip onto its own track.
  • Cinematic Beats: Dramatic, high-impact background scores designed for travel vlogs, action sequences, and trailers.
  • Corporate & Info Music: Clean, subtle background tracks suited for business presentations, tutorials, and educational videos.
  • Upbeat Vlog Sounds: Energetic, rhythmic tracks tailored to keep viewers engaged on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and vertical feeds.
  • Chill Lofi Loops: Relaxed, ambient beats that set a calm mood for slow-motion edits, daily vlogs, or spoken commentary.

4. Overlay Motion, Transform & Precision Controls

Motion Animations: Apply smooth entrance, exit, and transition effects to text, stickers, and video overlays.
Keyframe Tracking: Animate graphics, text, or facecams along custom motion paths across the screen.
Transform Lock: Lock an element's position and size so it does not shift accidentally while you edit other tracks.
Duplicate Layer: Create an exact copy of any text block, sticker, or overlay—including its styling and effects—in one click.
Precision Crop & Shape Masks: Trim unwanted edges or frame clips into circles, rounded cards, and split-screen layouts.
Duration Timer: Set exact start and end timestamps so overlays sync accurately with your spoken audio.
AI Cutout & Chroma Key: Isolate people, remove solid backgrounds, or key out green screens in one step.
Rotate, Flip & Mirror Effect: Adjust angles freely, rotate by 90°/180°, or mirror clips horizontally and vertically for symmetrical shots.
Auto Fit & Social Safe Zones: Resize clips to 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 instantly while checking Reels/Shorts UI safe margins.
Layer Blur & Privacy Boxes: Soften overlay edges for depth-of-field or add movable blur masks over sensitive details.
Speed Control & Freeze Frame: Dial playback from 0.25x smooth slow-motion up to 2.0x fast-forward, or hold any frame as a still.
Quick Purge & History Reset: Clear unused layers, step backward with Undo/Redo, and keep your timeline organized.

5. Color Grading, Visual Effects & Thumbnail Capture

  • Real-Time Color Adjustments: Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, and warmth or apply one-click Cinematic, Vintage, and Noir looks.
  • Transitions & Visual Overlays: Smoothly bridge scene changes with Fade, Pop Zoom, Slide, and Wipe transitions, or layer Vignette and Retro VHS textures.
  • Instant Frame Snapshot: Capture any high-resolution frame from your video timeline as a ready-to-use PNG thumbnail.

6. Exporting & Local Project Management

  • Flexible Export Resolutions & FPS: Export your finished project in 480p, 720p, 1080p Full HD, or 1440p (2K) at 24, 30, or 60 fps—or save as an animated GIF or standalone MP3 audio file.
  • Optimized File Sizes: Built-in bitrate controls let you balance sharp visual clarity against smaller file sizes, complete with live file-size estimates before downloading.
  • Zero Watermarks: Deliver clean, client-ready videos and transparent PNGs without forced logos, trial limits, or branding overlays.
  • Device Auto-Save Drafts: Your active edits save locally in browser storage so you can close the tab and pick up right where you left off from Saved Drafts.
  • Tutorials & Learning Resources: Explore practical editing guides and workflow tips anytime in our Tutorials & Blog section.
