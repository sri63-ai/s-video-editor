Local Browser Storage Notice Device-Dependent Cache • Best for Lightweight Projects

Your drafts are saved locally inside your browser's storage, and the available capacity depends on your device's free disk space and browser limits—making it best suited for lightweight and medium-sized projects.

Because local browser cache is not a permanent cloud backup and may be cleared automatically by your browser or device cleanup tools, please avoid relying 100% on saved drafts and try to complete and export your project within the same day.
