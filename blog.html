The Mechanics of In-Browser Media Processing for Video and Photos

Retention Mechanics & Pacing

Vary Visuals with Dynamic Layering for Peak Retention

Overcoming static talking-head drop-offs with scale modulation, 4–6 second visual shifts, B-roll overlays, and contextual lower-thirds.

Implementation Architecture: Dynamic Layering for Visual Retention

Static talking-head shots cause audience engagement to drop quickly. Introducing visual rhythm by shifting on-screen composition every 4 to 6 seconds preserves pacing and viewer retention.

  • Scale Modulation (Punch-Ins): Switch between Wide (100%) and Medium Tight (115%–120%) on key points.
  • 4–6 Second Rhythm: Alternate between Primary Shot, B-roll Overlay, and Punch-In Return.
  • Animated Lower-Thirds: Highlight key takeaways in the lower third during the first 2 seconds of a new point.
